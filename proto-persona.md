# Proto Persona

## Purpose

Vínculo operates in a social and creative context where users interact with the system in different ways.

These proto personas are informed by observed patterns from interviews and conversations with artists, researchers, and collaborators working across creative communities.

Across these conversations, recurring themes emerged:
- social platforms reward performance over participation  
- collaboration is often invisible or unevenly credited  
- people feel present in communities but not structurally represented  
- meaningful connections are formed through shared work, not visibility  

These personas translate those patterns into clear user types to guide documentation and design decisions.

---

## Persona 1 — The Observer

**Description**

A person who participates in creative or intellectual work but does not actively present themselves on social platforms.

**Characteristics**

- Rarely posts or shares publicly  
- Avoids performative online identity  
- Engages with communities through reading, watching, and participating in projects  
- Values privacy and control over visibility  

**Behavior**

- Engages deeply, but quietly  
- Understands networks through context and shared work  
- Recognizes people and projects, but is not visibly connected online  

**Pain points**

- Existing platforms reward visibility rather than contribution  
- Their work and collaborations are not legible or structured  
- Feels present in communities but not represented  

**Needs**

- A way to see their position in a network without self-promotion  
- A system that reflects participation rather than activity  
- Recognition through contribution, not performance  

**Relevance to Vínculo**

Vínculo allows this user to appear in the network through shared work, without requiring them to construct a public-facing identity.

They are included because they participated, not because they posted.

---

## Persona 2 — The Contributor

**Description**

A person who actively collaborates across projects and roles within creative or intellectual communities.

**Characteristics**

- Works with multiple collaborators across different contexts  
- Takes on varied roles (editor, organizer, contributor, etc.)  
- Participates in distributed or informal communities  

**Behavior**

- Regularly contributes to projects and organizations  
- Tracks relationships through shared work rather than formal networks  
- Values collaboration and collective production  

**Pain points**

- Credit is uneven and often centralized around one visible figure  
- Collaboration history is fragmented across platforms  
- No system represents the full structure of shared work  

**Needs**

- A way to credit collaborators accurately  
- A system that reflects roles and contributions  
- A visible structure of how work connects people over time  

**Relevance to Vínculo**

Vínculo enables this user to document and reveal collaboration by naming participants and roles, making the network of shared work visible.

It reflects how work is actually made — collectively.

---

## Key insight

Both personas are already part of a network, but that network is not visible in existing systems.

Vínculo reveals connections through shared participation in real projects and organizations.
