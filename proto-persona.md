# Proto Persona — VITRO

## Sofia Reyes
*Head of Retail, mid-size European cultural institution*

### Brief bio
Sofia is 38, has worked in museum retail for 8 years, and manages 
a shop team of 4. She has a background in arts administration, not 
data or tech. She is comfortable with Excel and uses gomus daily 
but has never worked with a BI tool. She cares deeply about the 
shop performing well and is the person leadership turns to when 
revenue targets aren't met.

### Interests
Sofia is interested in understanding which exhibitions drive shop 
sales, which products resonate with which visitor types, and how 
to plan inventory more confidently before major openings.

### Needs
Clear, plain-language insights she can act on without needing a 
data analyst. A single place to see shop performance in context 
of visitor behavior — without exporting from three different tools 
every Monday morning.

### Alternatives currently in use
gomus for POS and ticketing. Google Analytics for web. Excel for 
combining everything. Gut instinct for the rest.

### Attitudes, values, goals
Sofia is pragmatic and time-poor. She trusts data when it's 
presented clearly but is skeptical of tools that promise too much. 
Her goal is to prove the shop's value to leadership with real 
numbers — and to stop spending two hours every week just pulling 
reports together.
