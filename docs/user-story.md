# User Stories

## How these were built

Each story below is anchored in one of the two proto personas (see `personas.md`). The personas were synthesized from the TCI interview series, six long-form conversations with artists, researchers, and collaborators working across creative communities. The user stories follow the language of those conversations: shared work, participation, credit, traces.

---

## Story 1 — Entering the network

**Persona:** The Observer

**Story**

As someone who does not actively present themselves online,
I want to see myself already connected to work I have participated in,
so that I can understand my place in a network without having to build a profile.

**Context**

The Observer has been named in a project by a collaborator. They receive an invite.

**Flow**

- Opens the invite link
- Lands on a map where they are already present as a node
- Sees the project they were part of, and the people connected to it
- Explores their immediate network without having to introduce themselves

**Outcome**

The user recognizes themselves in the system without performing or constructing an identity. They are included because they participated, not because they posted.

---

## Story 2 — Expanding the network

**Persona:** The Contributor

**Story**

As someone who works with others across projects,
I want to add a project and name everyone involved,
so that collaboration is properly credited and the people who shape my work become visible.

**Context**

The Contributor has claimed their node and has access to the full map. They are starting to add work they have done.

**Flow**

- Adds a new project or organization
- Selects its type
- Names participants and assigns roles (lead, contributor, collaborator)
- Submits the node

**Outcome**

The network expands through real collaboration. Collaborators who never had a profile become visible nodes through the act of being named. The web of shared work grows by the people who actually do it.

---

## Story 3 — Understanding a connection

**Persona:** Both

**Story**

As someone navigating the network,
I want to understand how I am connected to another person,
so that I can see my relationship to them through the work that connects us.

**Context**

The user is exploring the map from their own node, or from someone else's.

**Flow**

- Selects another person in the network
- Views the trace, the path connecting them through shared projects and organizations
- Reads the editorial explanation of how the connection formed

**Outcome**

The user understands their relationship to others through real participation, not through followers, endorsements, or inferred similarity. The connection is shown, not declared.
